Typical Water Damage Problems in North Charleston

Water trouble is not all equally urgent, and knowing the difference helps you decide what to do first. We have ordered these five common North Charleston calls by how fast each one needs attention.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Depending on the season, the break may follow a freeze, and it often shows only when the ice inside melts. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call right away, even at night. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Rain running into the house at ground level ranks a close second. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. The Florence Restoration team extracts it once conditions are safe, then removes soaked materials that cannot be cleaned.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. For North Charleston kitchen jobs, our crew can frequently save the cabinets by pushing dry air in at the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Moisture under a raised floor is seldom an emergency, but it should not be put off for a season. When we look underneath, we tend to find water lying on the liner, insulation that is soaked or sweating ducts. Left alone, the damp air rises into the rooms above and the wood framing begins to decay. We extract the water, dry the wood to a safe reading and tell you what we think the source is.

Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Avoid wiping or bleaching a big area on your own, as that sends spores into other rooms. We check for hidden dampness, set up containment and clear out the affected material for homeowners in North Charleston.

Insurance and Water Damage: A Guide for North Charleston Owners
